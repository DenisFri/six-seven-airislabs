---
name: quiz
description: Play the skeptical client - challenge one design decision at a time and grade the answers.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

1. Pick one item from "Decisions and trade-offs" in `docs/architecture.md` that was not asked in this session.
2. Ask it the way a skeptical client would, in one sentence. Wait for the answer.
3. Grade: strong / incomplete / wrong. Then give the strong answer in one short paragraph, including the stricter alternative.
4. Repeat. Keep a scoreboard and write it to `docs/drills.md` at the end.
5. Also use the "Think about" list in `docs/build-log.md` and the sizing question (RAM vs working set, connections, disk growth factors, IOPS by disk type).
